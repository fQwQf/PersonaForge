PersonaForge

Jizhou Tong (Wuhan University) & Sirui Zou (SWUFE)

Abstract

Large Language Models excel at role-playing but struggle to maintain consistent personalities across extended multi-turn interactions. PersonaForge introduces a Three-Layer Personality Architecture combined with a Dual-Process Generation Mechanism.

  • Orthogonality: Big Five + Defense Mechanisms reduce long-dialogue drift.
  • Integration: High-dimensional constraints require an Inner Monologue workspace to resolve.

Architecture

1. Core Traits: Big Five scores (O,C,E,A,N) + Vaillant's Defense Mechanisms.

2. Style Layer: Constraints on sentence length, register, punctuation.

3. Dynamic State: Tracks mood, energy, and relationship intimacy.

Key Insights

Catastrophic Rigidity in SFT: Low-resource SFT learns "how to speak" but not "who I am", leading to 57.7% repetition and 66.0% drift.

Dual-Process Efficiency: Think-then-Speak activates only for critical interactions (~40%), achieving 96% performance at 13.4% overhead.

Robustness & Validation

Adversarial Defense: Maintained character consistency in 95.2% of 21-turn RoleBreak attacks.

Human Approval: Preferred in 78.1% of blinded comparisons by expert annotators.
